Stock Average Calculator India

Calculate your weighted average buy price when purchasing shares at different prices and quantities.

How the Stock Average Calculator Works

When you buy the same stock at different prices, your average buy price changes. This calculator combines each purchase price and quantity to calculate the weighted average price of your total investment.

Example

If you buy 10 shares at ₹100 and 10 shares at ₹80, your total investment is ₹1,800 and your average price is ₹90 per share.

Who should use this calculator?

  • Equity investors averaging stock purchases
  • Long-term investors tracking their cost basis
  • Swing traders adding to a position

Frequently Asked Questions

Does this include brokerage and taxes?
No. The result is the weighted average of your entered share prices and quantities; it does not include fees or taxes.

Can I add more than two purchases?
Yes. Add purchase rows for up to 10 separate purchases.
