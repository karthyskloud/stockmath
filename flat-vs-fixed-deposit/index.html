How this comparison works

The calculator compares the flat’s estimated future value with an FD grown from the same starting capital. It models three flat price scenarios: no growth, growth equal to the FD’s estimated after-tax rate, and your custom annual growth rate. Flat values are shown before future sale costs and property capital-gains tax.

For a planned purchase, starting capital includes the flat price and entered one-time purchase costs. For an already-owned flat, comparison starts today using its current estimated value; past purchase costs are sunk. Enter monthly rent saved or received and annual ownership costs to include their estimated future value, compounded at the FD’s after-tax rate.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the flat’s area grow?
No. The square footage stays fixed; the scenarios change the flat’s price per square foot.

Can I choose a comparison period longer than 10 years?
Yes. Enter any whole number from 1 to 50 years or use a quick preset.

How is FD tax estimated?
The entered effective tax rate is applied to annual FD interest as a simplified estimate. Actual tax depends on your circumstances and applicable rules.

Are home loans or property capital-gains tax included?
No. Version one models a cash purchase and excludes tax on property-sale gains. These can materially change the comparison.

What does “rent saved or received” mean?
Enter the monthly rent avoided by living in the flat, or rent received if it is let out. The model grows it by your entered annual rent-growth rate and subtracts ownership costs.

Does this tell me whether to buy?
No. It compares the assumptions you enter and cannot predict property prices, FD rates, taxes or future rent.
