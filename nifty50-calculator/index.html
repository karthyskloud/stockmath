What If You Had Invested in NIFTY 50?

This calculator shows how much your money would be worth today if you had invested equally in all NIFTY 50 companies on any past date.

Example: 2020-03-23
If empty, latest available prices will be used
1 = Buy 1 share of each NIFTY 50 company

✨ Try this: Select 2020-03-23 (COVID crash day) and enter 1 share to see how much you would have today.

Note: If the selected date is a market holiday, the nearest previous trading day is used.
