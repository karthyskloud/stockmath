How to read the scenarios

The planned-stop result assumes an exit at your stop price. The gap result assumes an exit at your entered gap price. The optional circuit result assumes an exit at your entered circuit scenario price. All losses are estimates for a long equity position.

  • Planned loss: entry-to-stop loss for your current shares.
  • Gap loss: estimated loss if the position exits below the planned stop at your assumed price.
  • Circuit loss: a separate stress scenario at the price you enter.
  • Shares within budget: estimated maximum shares for each scenario, before rounding to exchange/broker constraints.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does a stop-loss guarantee my exit price?
No. A stop order may execute at a different price if the market gaps or liquidity is limited.

Does this predict gaps or circuit moves?
No. It only calculates scenarios from prices you enter.

Can I use a circuit price from any stock?
Yes, as a manual scenario. Verify the current price band with your exchange or broker; bands can vary and change.

Are brokerage and taxes included?
Only if you enter them in estimated round-trip charges. This is not a tax calculation, and actual charges may differ.

Does this calculate short positions or options?
No. This version models a long equity position only.
