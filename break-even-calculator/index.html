Break-Even & Target Sell Price Calculator

Find the minimum price to sell your shares at break-even or to reach a target net profit after estimated trading charges.

Enter 0 to calculate your break-even sell price.

Enter your estimated round-trip brokerage, or leave as 0.

How it works

The calculator estimates the sell price needed to cover your buy value, target profit, entered brokerage and estimated statutory charges. The displayed sell price is rounded up to the next paisa so it does not fall below the calculated target.

Charges included

  • STT on buy and sell turnover
  • Stamp duty on buy turnover
  • Exchange transaction charges
  • SEBI turnover charges
  • GST on exchange and SEBI charges

Statutory charge assumptions match StockMath’s Trading Charges Calculator. Actual charges can differ by trade type, exchange, broker and current regulations. Brokerage is based on the amount you enter.

Example

Enter your buy price and share quantity, then leave target net profit at ₹0 to estimate the break-even sell price. Enter a positive target to see the minimum estimated sell price for that profit after charges.
