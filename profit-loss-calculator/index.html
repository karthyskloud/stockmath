How Profit and Loss is Calculated

Profit or loss is calculated by subtracting the total buy value from the total sell value. This calculator also shows percentage returns on your investment.

Example

Buying 50 shares at ₹100 and selling at ₹120 gives a profit of ₹1000 with a return of 20%.
