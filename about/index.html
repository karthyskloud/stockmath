About StockMath

StockMath is a simple and fast set of trading calculators designed for Indian stock market traders.

Our goal is to help traders quickly calculate averages, profits, losses, position sizes, stock splits, and trading charges without complex tools or registrations.

StockMath does not provide financial advice and does not require user accounts. All calculators are free to use and work directly in your browser.

We believe trading tools should be easy, transparent, and accessible to everyone.
