Position Size Calculator India

Calculate how many shares you should buy based on your total capital, risk per trade, entry price and stop-loss.

Why Position Size Matters

Position sizing helps control risk by limiting how much capital you risk in a trade. This calculator shows how many shares you should buy based on your stop loss.

Example

With ₹1,00,000 capital and 1% risk, you risk only ₹1000 per trade.
