Stock Average Calculator India

This stock average calculator helps Indian traders calculate their average buy price when purchasing shares at different prices and quantities.

How the Stock Average Calculator Works

When you buy the same stock at different prices, your average buy price changes. This calculator combines multiple buy prices and quantities to calculate the weighted average price of your total investment.

Example

If you buy 10 shares at ₹100 and 10 shares at ₹80, your total investment is ₹1800 and your average price becomes ₹90.

Who should use this calculator?

  • Equity investors averaging stocks
  • Long-term investors reducing cost
  • Swing traders managing positions

FAQs

Yes, for quick average calculations.
